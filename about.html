About

I'm an economist based in Munich, finishing a second master's in European Economic Studies in Bamberg. My path runs from a BA at Boğaziçi University through an MSc at LMU Munich, with research stops at the World Trade Organization and Allianz Research along the way.

I like turning messy real-world data — trade flows, inflation, financial markets — into something useful. My master's thesis used LSTM models to forecast inflation surprises; lately that same instinct has pulled me toward building AI tools for economic research.

About

I studied economics in Istanbul, then Munich — a path that took me from undergraduate coursework at Boğaziçi University to a master's at LMU Munich, by way of a semester in Paris. Along the way I picked up research experience that's hard to plan for in advance: harmonizing tariff data at the World Trade Organization in Geneva, building inflation dashboards and recession models at Allianz Research, and running experiments and econometric analysis for two research chairs at LMU.

What ties it together is an interest in taking messy, real-world data — trade flows, inflation surprises, financial markets — and turning it into something that actually says something. My master's thesis used LSTM models to forecast inflation surprises and trace their effects on US trade flows; more recently I've been applying that same instinct to building AI tools for economic research.

I'm currently in Bamberg finishing a second master's in European Economic Studies, based out of Munich.

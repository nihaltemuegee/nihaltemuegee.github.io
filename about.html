About

I'm a quantitative economist based in Munich, with an MSc from LMU Munich and experience in financial econometrics, statistical modeling, time-series analysis, and machine learning — developed through research at Allianz Research, LMU Munich, and the World Trade Organization.

My research centers on macroeconomic forecasting and applied econometrics. I'm interested in how inflation dynamics, monetary policy, and international trade flows interact, and in how modern forecasting methods — from time-series models to machine learning — can improve how we understand and predict them.

Current and recent projects include an LSTM-based framework for forecasting inflation surprises and their effects on US trade flows (my master's thesis), statistical and forecasting models for inflation and recession risk at Allianz Research, work harmonizing cross-country tariff data at the WTO, and an AI research assistant that applies retrieval-augmented generation to economics research.

About

I'm a development-oriented economist with international research experience at the World Trade Organization, Allianz Research, and LMU Munich. I specialize in macroeconomic analysis, international trade, economic policy, financial markets, forecasting, and applied econometrics — with experience working on large-scale cross-country datasets and translating quantitative findings into policy-relevant insights.

I'm proficient in Python, R, SQL, Stata, and Power BI, with additional expertise in machine learning and AI-enabled analytical workflows — including LSTM forecasting, NLP, LLMs, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
